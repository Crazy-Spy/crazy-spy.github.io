The Mission

The world has gone dark. The global power grid has collapsed under a massive, unprecedented cyber attack.

As an elite network engineer, your mission is critical: dive into high-tension logic puzzles to reconnect the nodes, reroute power, and bring the grid back online. Every move matters.
